Non-negotiable

The rules

These govern every draft, every channel. A draft that breaks one comes back.

  1. One job per asset. Define, diagnose, apply, prove, or convert. Never two.
  2. One CTA per asset. Exactly one. Secondary routes are plain text links, never a second button.
  3. Lead with the point. No setup. The first sentence is the strongest sentence.
  4. One mantra per asset. Underline the one sentence a CEO should still remember tomorrow. If nothing is underlineable, it is not done.
  5. No em dashes. Client standard. Use a period, a comma, or a colon.
  6. The firm is the actor. Blue Lake Ideas owns the system. Matt Elliott is founder and principal voice — used for proof and story, not as the subject of every sentence.
  7. Owned terms exactly as written. No synonyms, no improvements. ™ on first use per page only.
  8. Solution is not Method. The Power of &™ is what to do. The & Intelligence™ Framework is how you bring it to life, in three steps. The framework folds under & Intelligence.
Banned on sight

Unlock · transform · bespoke · holistic · leverage as a verb · "in today's fast-paced world" · "bring your whole self" · both/and thinking · find common ground · slow down · experiment · any consulting phrase that could appear on another firm's website.

Who you are writing for

A CEO or board member who is already overloaded. They will give you ninety seconds and they are reading on a phone between meetings. Respect their capacity on the surface. Give search engines and AI answer engines the depth underneath.
