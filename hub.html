The 5 Canonical Terms

These are the owned vocabulary. Every piece of content — web, Substack, LinkedIn, YouTube, email — uses these exact terms. No synonyms. No improvisation.

⚠️ The two terms writers keep confusing: The Power of &™ is the framework (three steps). & Intelligence™ is the capability (fluency to run those steps under pressure). They are not interchangeable. The homepage was recently corrected for this exact mistake.
Term — use exactly thisLayerOne-line definitionNever call it
The Binary TrapMechanismThe pattern where accelerating change consumes leadership capacity, fast defaults take over, and interdependent priorities get recast as either/or choices.The Causal Chain, binary thinking, complexity
The Four TaxesCostWhat organizations pay when the Binary Trap becomes the operating pattern: False Choices, Silos, Short-Termism, Stalled Innovation.Costs, consequences, downsides. Always name all four.
The Power of &™FrameworkThree steps that let leaders hold real tensions over time: Pause & Reflect, Find the &, Test & Learn. Never call it the framework alone.Both/and thinking, yes/and, Power of And
& Intelligence™CapabilityThe developed capability to run the framework under pressure. Different from the framework itself — it is the fluency to execute it when the stakes are high.The training, the method, the model

Three sentences every writer must know

1. The Binary Trap is what happens to leaders under load. The Four Taxes are what it costs the enterprise.

2. The Power of &™ is the framework and its three steps. & Intelligence™ is the fluency to run it under pressure.

3. The Capacity Advantage is what you get. Speaking, Facilitator Certification, and Advisory are three depths of the same system.

90-Day Sequence

Ownership first, conversion in parallel. Do not skip steps — each window unlocks the next.

Days 1–15 · Govern

Fix the definition conflict across all live assets. Corrected homepage with The Binary Trap named. One primary CTA. Add visible FAQ + JSON-LD.

Days 16–30 · Define

Pillar 1: & Intelligence™ (2,000–2,500 words, 7 FAQs). Pillar 2: The Binary Trap. Sub-pillar: The Four Taxes.

Days 31–45 · Convert

The Capacity Audit (10-question diagnostic). Chapter one capture page. HubSpot properties, lifecycle stages, and 4 core workflows.

Days 46–60 · Define+

Pillar 3: The Capacity Advantage. Pillar 4: The Power of &™. Rewrite Advisory page against new spec.

Days 61–75 · Apply

Six cluster articles (min. one per pillar). Migrate strongest Substack and LinkedIn pieces into permanent Insights posts.

Days 76–90 · Prove

Three proof cases. Speaking and Certification pages rewritten. First conversion report from HubSpot.

If only three things get done

Fix the definition conflict. Name The Binary Trap on the homepage. Build the Capacity Audit. These three moves fix credibility, vocabulary ownership, and the absence of a funnel.

Integration

Web / Substack / Social / YouTube

One system, four channels. The website is always canonical. Every other channel points back to it.

🌐

Website (bluelakeideas.com)

The canonical home for every owned term. The pillar page is the deepest, most authoritative source. Every other channel links here.

  • One definition per term, one page per term
  • FAQ + JSON-LD on every pillar
  • The definition ships before or same day as social
  • Canonical tags on all migrated content
✉️

Substack

Long-form essays that originate here migrate to /insights for permanent ownership. Original Substack post updates with a link to the web version.

  • Never delete the original Substack post
  • Add: "The canonical version of this piece lives at [URL]"
  • Strong Substack pieces → migrate to Insights within 30 days
  • Substack audience → chapter one capture
💼

LinkedIn + YouTube

Shorter form, single idea, one CTA per post. Always leads back to the web for the full context.

  • Lead with the owned term (first 2 lines)
  • One CTA per post, always a web URL
  • YouTube descriptions include the pillar URL
  • LinkedIn posts drive to the relevant Insights article or pillar
  • Never define a term in full on social — drive to the pillar
The cardinal rule

A social post can introduce attention. It cannot be the only place the definition lives. The website definition ships first, or at minimum the same day as the social launch.

Non-Negotiable Rules

10 Rules That Cannot Break

Every draft, every channel, every publish. These are not preferences.

  • 1. One term, one canonical page. No term is defined in two places. Everything links to the single source.
  • 2. The Binary Trap is the mechanism. Never use "The Causal Chain," "binary thinking," or "complexity" as substitutes.
  • 3. The Power of &™ ≠ & Intelligence™. Framework vs. capability. These are not interchangeable.
  • 4. One CTA per page. Exactly one. Secondary routes are plain text links only — no second button.
  • 5. The Binary Trap appears on the homepage. By name. Above the fold. Not buried in section three.
  • 6. Cluster links up, pillar links down. Every cluster page links to its pillar. Every pillar links to its clusters. Orphaned pages do not rank.
  • 7. No em dashes. Ever. Use a period, a comma, or a colon instead.
  • 8. Clarity. Connection. Courage. Three parts. This order. Periods. Never reordered or extended.
  • 9. FAQ + JSON-LD on every substantive page. Visible, complete-sentence answers. Matches schema exactly. This is the AEO foundation.
  • 10. Website definition ships first. Before or same day as any social or email launch. A post can generate attention. It cannot be the only place the definition lives.

Also Banned on Sight

"In today's fast-paced world" · "Unlock" · "Transform" · "Bespoke" · "Holistic" · "Leverage" (as a verb) · "Both/and thinking" (as a Power of &™ substitute) · "Find common ground" (for Find the &) · "Slow down" (for Pause & Reflect) · "Experiment" (for Test & Learn)
