The five owned terms what they mean, what never to substitute
The two writers keep confusing

The Power of &™ is the Solution: hold the tension. The & Intelligence™ Framework is the Method: the three steps that run it. The steps live under & Intelligence, at /and-intelligence/*.

Mechanism
The Binary Trap

Accelerating change consumes leadership capacity, fast defaults take over, and interdependent priorities get recast as either/or choices.

Never: The Causal Chain, binary thinking, complexity
Cost
The Four Taxes

What organizations pay when the trap becomes the operating pattern: False Choices, Silos, Short-Termism, Stalled Innovation.

Never: costs, consequences, downsides. Name all four on first use.
Solution
The Power of &™

Hold real tensions together over time against a shared definition of success, instead of resolving them into either/or. States what to do, not how to run it.

Never: both/and thinking, yes/and, Power of And, "the framework"
Method
& Intelligence™ Framework

The three steps that operationalize the solution: Pause & Reflect (Clarity), Find the & (Connection), Test & Learn (Courage). The deepest pillar on the site.

Never: the training, the capability, the model
Outcome
The Capacity Advantage

What leaders gain when they recapture usable capacity and preserve strategic range: better decisions, better delivery, sustained performance.

Never: resilience, bandwidth, wellness, capacity building

The ten rules not preferences
  1. One term, one canonical page. No term is defined in two places.
  2. The Binary Trap is the mechanism. Never "The Causal Chain," "binary thinking," or "complexity."
  3. Solution is not Method. The Power of &™ is what to do. The & Intelligence™ Framework is how to run it.
  4. One CTA per page. Secondary routes are plain text links, never a second button.
  5. The Binary Trap appears above the fold on the homepage. By name.
  6. Cluster links up, pillar links down. Orphaned pages do not rank.
  7. No em dashes. A period, a comma, or a colon.
  8. Clarity. Connection. Courage. Three parts, this order, periods. Never extended.
  9. FAQ and JSON-LD on every substantive page. Visible answers match the schema exactly.
  10. The website definition ships first. Before or the same day as social.

Banned on sight: unlock · transform · bespoke · holistic · leverage as a verb · "in today's fast-paced world" · both/and thinking · find common ground · slow down · experiment

Publish gate check before anything ships
  • Primary owned term appears in the headline or opening section
  • One content job only: define, diagnose, apply, prove, or convert
  • Definitions match the five canonical terms exactly
  • Solution and Method treated as distinct
  • All four taxes named on first introduction
  • Clarity. Connection. Courage. in order, three parts only
  • No em dashes anywhere in the draft
  • Exactly one CTA, mapped to a lifecycle stage
  • Cluster page links to its pillar with exact-term anchor text
  • Every owned term links to its canonical page on first mention only
  • SEO title under 60 characters, owned term front-loaded
  • Meta description 150 to 160 characters
  • Two to three visible FAQs with matching JSON-LD
  • One underlineable sentence a CEO would remember tomorrow
  • Command Center updated with URL and publish date
