Decisions

Both come from your buyer chain meeting the site architecture. Neither is urgent this week, but both block Wave 1 copy.

Decision 01 · Highest consequence

Do the three steps belong to & Intelligence™?

Your chain puts Pause & Reflect, Find the &, and Test & Learn under the & Intelligence™ Framework as the Method, with The Power of &™ standing alone as the Solution. An earlier direction had them under The Power of &™.

What we have built to: your version. The steps live at /and-intelligence/*, & Intelligence becomes the deepest page on the site at 2,000 to 2,500 words, and The Power of &™ becomes a 1,200-word belief page.

Why it needs a yes in writing: it decides which page is deepest, which URL the steps live at, and which term every internal link points to. Changing it after launch means redirects and starting the authority clock over.

Decision 02

Workshops, Certification, or both?

Your Tools layer lists Workshops. The current build has & Intelligence™ Facilitator Certification. These are different products: a workshop is delivered to a team, a certification is earned by a facilitator. Different buyers, different price points.

Our recommendation: keep both, on separate pages. If only one ships first, ship Workshops — shorter sales cycle. Certification is the long game: certified facilitators repeating your language is the highest-quality inbound the firm can generate.
